💡 How it works

One list, one uniform

You met and, but, or joining clauses. Here they join smaller pieces — and demand that every piece have the same grammatical shape: all nouns, all infinitives, all clauses. Load a sentence: each item sits on its own bar, labelled with its form. An item in a different form tilts its bar ✘; Level it repairs the sentence. (Los elementos de una lista deben compartir la misma forma gramatical.)

📍 Rule of position

The paired conjunctions — bracket the true alternatives

both … andboth the cost and the schedule
either … oreither redesign the part or reinforce it
neither … norneither confirmed nor denied
not only … but alsonot only faster but also cheaper
whether … orwhether by rail or by road

What follows the first word must match what follows the second. If the halves bracket different shapes, do not rewrite the second item — move the first half. Tap a slot ▢ to place it. (Mueve la primera mitad del par; no reescribas la segunda.)
