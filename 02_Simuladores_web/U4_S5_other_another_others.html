💡 How it works

Two questions decide everything

Ask how many? (one or more than one) and which ones? (any more of them, or exactly the ones that are left). Those two answers select one of the five forms. (Dos preguntas: singular o plural, y si hablamos de «los que quedan» — eso pide the.)

💡 The two hard rules

another = an + other · other needs a noun, others stands alone

  • another already contains an: it takes a singular noun and never takes the. ✘ the another · ✘ an another · ✘ another tests → ✔ another test
  • other on its own (no the) is an adjective: it always has a noun after it. ✔ other tests · ✔ other equipment ✘ Some passed; other failed. (Con the sí puede ir solo: the other.)
  • others is a pronoun: it replaces the noun, so nothing follows it. ✔ Some passed; others failed. ✘ others tests
