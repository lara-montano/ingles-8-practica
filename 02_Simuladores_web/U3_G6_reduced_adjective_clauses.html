💡 How it works

The Reduction Press: full clause → reduced clause

Good engineering removes material without removing strength. Academic English shrinks most adjective clauses the same way: the press deletes the connector and be and keeps the participle. It works only when the connector is the subject of its clause. Choose a pattern, then press — one step at a time. (Solo se reduce cuando el conector es el sujeto de su cláusula; se borran conector y «be».)
