    ⚙️ The three machines

    -er / more … than · as … as · the … the

    Each machine has its own parts, and the parts never mix. (Nunca as … than ni more … as.)

    🔗 The correlative comparative

    The … the — two halves, two thes

    One change drives another. Both halves open with the + comparative; drop either the and the structure collapses. (Las dos mitades llevan the.)

    🎚️ C1 refinement

    Calibrate the gap — the comparison intensifiers

    A bare comparative states a difference; these tools state its size. Each is fixed to one host: a comparative, a superlative, or as … as. (Cada intensificador elige a su anfitrión.)

    Trap: very cheaper, very more durable — very refuses comparatives. Use much or far.

    🚨 Error clinic

    What Spanish speakers get wrong here

    more better · more high → better · higherNever stack more onto a comparative; short adjectives take -er.
    as cheap than · more cheap as → as cheap as · cheaper thanThe machines never swap parts.
    very cheaper → much / far cheaperVery refuses comparatives. (Muy más barato no existe.)
    The longer the span, deeper the beam → …, the deeper the beamBoth halves of the … the need the.
    higher than Plant B → higher than that of Plant BAn output is not higher than a plant. (Falta el de.)
    than the one of the department · smaller than the department → than that of the departmentNever the one of, and never a bare owner on side B. (No compares un sustantivo con un poseedor.)
    the tallest building of the city · the most best of all → in the city · the best of allin for a place, of for a set — and one superlative marker only.
    Of the two adhesives, the strongest → …, the strongerTwo items take the + comparative.
