💡 How it works

Four families, one rule: connector + subject + verb

Time and cause were the first half of the adverb-clause family. Here is the rest of the toolbox: condition, contrast, manner and place. Every one of them takes a full clause. Pick a family, then a connector, and read its meaning, its example and where the comma goes. (Cuatro familias; todas exigen una cláusula completa.)

The comma rule

Adverb clause first → comma. Adverb clause second → no comma. (La coma solo cuando la cláusula va delante.)

🔀 Swap the family

Same two ideas, different joint — different meaning

A condition connector makes idea B depend on idea A, which is not a fact yet. A contrast connector states A as a fact — and B happens anyway. Toggle the joint and watch the logic (and the tense) change. (Mismas ideas; el conector decide la lógica.)
