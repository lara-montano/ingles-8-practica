💡 How it works

Three words, three grammars — two slots

The sentence offers two openings: before a noun (a preposition slot) and after the verb (a predicate slot, with nothing following). Each word fits one of them. Pick a word and the machine ticks the slot it can take and crosses out the other. (Alike solo va después del verbo; like y unlike piden un sustantivo.)

💡 The like / as switch

What comes after the gap decides the word

Change the complement and the machine changes the word. Ask one question: is there a subject + verb after the gap? (Con sustantivo, like; con cláusula, as; «en calidad de», as.)

⚖️ Unlike — the balance

Unlike must contrast comparable things

Unlike is a preposition: unlike + noun. And the noun it takes must belong to the same category as the noun it is contrasted with. (Compara peras con peras.)
