💡 How it works

Cause wears -ing; experiencer wears -ed

One verb, two adjectives. -ing = producing the effect; -ed = experiencing it. Pick a verb, then throw the switch: the same feeling changes owner and the sentence changes ending. (El que causa lleva -ing; el que siente lleva -ed.)

    🌉 The participle bridge

    This is Unit 3 wearing feelings

    The manual is explicit: -ing does, -ed is done to. An -ing adjective is the active participle of a reduced clause; an -ed adjective is the passive one. Expand it back and the answer appears. (Misma regla de las cláusulas reducidas de la Unit 3.)
