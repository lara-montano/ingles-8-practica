💡 How it works

The division of labor

One Spanish verb, two English verbs. Ask one question: does something new exist afterwards? If yes, it is make. If an activity simply gets carried out, it is do. (Si al final hay algo nuevo, es make; si solo se ejecuta una actividad, es do.)

⭐ The C1 shelf

Fixed blocks — memorise them whole

The criterion will not decide these for you: they are single units, and they decide exam points. Learn the verb inside the block. (Bloques fijos: se memorizan enteros, con su verbo.)

The crossover pair: make do (arreglárselas) is MAKE; do without (prescindir) is DO. The exam loves putting them side by side.

🧭 Why hacer betrays you

One verb in Spanish, two in English

Spanish uses hacer for both jobs, so the interference is predictable: the exam writes the sentence that a Spanish speaker would translate word for word. These are the pairs that fail most. (El español no distingue; el examen ataja justo ahí.)
